Karen Lee Pieper, aged 63, passed away on August 6 of this year, surrounded by family.
She is survived by her children Samantha, Ian, and Clayton (me).

- Childhood
  - Born in April 26 1963 in Houston TX to Sam Pieper Jr and Joan Bruce Woods, two psychiatrists.
  - siblings John and David

- A woman in a man's world
  - Excelled in mathematics, physics, and computer science. Best student in 12 years
  - Junior ROTC. Crossed out "his" in the form

- University
  - One of the university's first CS grads

- Compilers
  - Ph.D. with John L. Hennessey on parallelizing compilers
  - After many detours, some of her work influenced modern compilers with SIMD vectorization
  - Stanford library could always had her thesis checked out

- Marriage
  - Met Donald Clayton Ramsey Jr, future husband, in grad school
  - He's even in her thesis acknowledgements

- Children
  - Samantha, Ian, Clayton, b. 1998, 2001, 2001
  - Practically a single mother at a young age

- Work on chip design
  - Synopsys
  - Chair of SystemVerilog, eventually just Verilog, committee for IEEE
  - Not a chip in the world without her work

- Separation, initial cancer diagnosis

- She got to see her kids graduate!

- Pivot to databases, leadership roles
  - Amazon, 2015
  - Facebook, 2017. Woodworking.

- Startups and senior leadership
  - Abundant robotics
  - Lightstep -> ServiceNow

- Retirement to Dripping Springs, Texas, 2022
  - Woodworking
  - Master naturalist
  - Raising her dogs

- Final cancer diagnosis, January 2025
  - Death in August of the next year
  - Tumors spread to the brain

- My own thoughts
  - Remembered for stubborn, headstrong nature and boundless generosity
